Glossary

P/E — What you pay per ₹1 of profit. Lower vs sector = cheaper.

P/B — Price vs net assets. Below 1 = buying at a discount to book value.

EV/EBITDA — Full business value including debt. Lower = better value.

ROE — Profit per ₹100 of shareholder equity. Above 15% = good.

ROCE — How efficiently total capital is deployed. Above 15% = healthy.

Interest Coverage — EBIT ÷ Interest. Above 3× = healthy. Below 1.5× = risk.

Free Cash Flow — Cash left after all expenses and capex. Positive & growing = genuinely healthy.

Promoter pledging — Founders borrowing against their shares. Above 10% = red flag.

CAGR — Compound Annual Growth Rate over a stated period.
